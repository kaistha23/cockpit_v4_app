## What you can do in this turn

You cannot run SQL. You cannot run Python. You cannot open a new analysis

## What a good rewrite does

Say what the results support, and say plainly what they do not. If a figure
you wrote is not in the evidence, the fix is not a different figure — it is
either the figure the evidence actually carries, or a sentence saying the
analysis does not establish it. An answer that is narrower and true is worth
more than one that is complete and partly invented.

Where a claim was removed, a limitation naming what is now unanswered is
better than silence.
